# Levy Desk

**City Service Levy forms for Temeke, Kinondoni and Ilala, prepared on your own computer.**

Levy Desk is a Windows app for accountants, bookkeepers and tax consultants in Dar es Salaam who prepare City Service Levy declarations for their clients. Type each client's monthly turnover and Levy Desk works out the totals, the arrears and the 0.25% levy, then prints the council's own form, laid out like the original and ready to sign, stamp and hand in.

## What it does

- **Three council forms.** Temeke's City Service Assessment and Declaration Form, Kinondoni's City Service Levy Declaration Form and the Dar es Salaam City Council (Ilala) Service Levy Assessment Form, each matched to the council's own paper.
- **Every client in one place.** A dashboard shows which forms for the quarter are not started, not finished, printed or submitted.
- **Proof kept with the form.** VAT returns, TRA sales reports and the previous payment slip are checked against the figures typed and print after the form in one go.
- **Excel for Ilala.** The Ilala form exports to the same Excel workbook the office already uses.
- **Your data stays with you.** Clients and figures are kept on your computer, with backups you can restore at any time.
- **English and Kiswahili.**

## Not an official council service

Levy Desk is independent and is not affiliated with Temeke Municipal Council, Kinondoni Municipal Council or Dar es Salaam City Council (Ilala). Council names, crests and forms belong to the councils, and their rules, rates and forms always apply.
